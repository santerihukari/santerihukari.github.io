## Practical designs

This collection brings together small designs made for real repairs and
everyday needs, including a replacement flash hot-shoe foot, a print-in-place
door lock, and a portable climbing fingerboard. Each model has a short
description, its current testing status, an interactive preview, and
downloadable STL files.

The models use different design tools, including Autodesk Fusion, OpenSCAD,
and Python with manifold3d. Some have been printed and tested in use; others
remain prototypes with limitations noted alongside their downloads.

The [model library]({{ '/stl/' | relative_url }}) is separate from the
[parametric model library]({{ '/stl_param/' | relative_url }}), which allows
parameters to be adjusted before generating and exporting geometry.
