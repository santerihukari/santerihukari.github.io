36 categories; 229 distinct listings reviewed; 109 candidate listings mapped to this assembly; 101 meshes have replacement/assembly/subcomponent candidates. ZERO confirmed fitted matches.

Mesh coverage is not a count of independently purchasable products. Related washers/nuts are not counted as replacement castings.

## Limits

- Catalogue research, not a confirmed shopping list. No VIN/registration or fitted component identities were supplied.
- Research targets the owner's stated vehicle configuration; editing CAD parameters does not automatically revalidate supplier fitment or the screening decisions.
- Supplier SKU is not a manufacturer/OEM number. Cross-references are examples, not identification of the fitted part.
- Some category cards combine alternative dates, VIN bounds, dimensions or applications without clear logic; verify with supplier.
- Assemblies, subcomponents and related hardware are counted separately. A gasket/nut is not a replacement casting.
- No prices, stock promises or delivery guarantees are frozen here. Some exchange parts may require a refundable core charge.
- Catalogue torque entries were not promoted to repair procedures. Exact joint, source conditions and applicability remain unverified.
- Only observed rendered cards are recorded; this is not an exhaustive Motonet inventory or a special-order availability statement.
